# SMC-Mini-Project
Alba's Miniproject for Sound and music Computing

--- Title ---

What does a person imagine and feel when listening to music?

--- Summary ---

The hypothesis of this study is that listeners form mental images while listening to music, and that the characteristics of these images correspond to the emotions experienced.

The study will involve AAU Copenhagen students who will listen to three different musical segments, which is different from each other. After each piece, participants will answer a questionnaire which inquires whether they experienced mental imagery, what kind of imagery they experienced, and the emotions they felt while listening.
