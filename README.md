# SMC-Mini-Project
Alba's Miniproject for Sound and music Computing

--- Title ---

Can music make people imagine stories and does those stories convey the same emotion and tempo for different people?

--- Summary ---

My hypothosis is that people's imagination sometimes builds stories when listening to music. Mine at least does and I don't think that this is unique only to me.

I wanna test 3-4 different musical pieces on people (AAU CPH students), and then ask them to answer a questonnaire about if they imagine things, or if they don't. What it does to their emotions those who imagine and not. Does imagination make music more enjoyable? 

I could also do a story-masking task where I give people several short story genres like action, romance, horror, and so on, and make people choose which best follow what they felt/had in mind when they listened to the music. 

I could also ask them if they remember certain points in the track that matched certain elements/tempo/feelings and try to get them to explain it.
